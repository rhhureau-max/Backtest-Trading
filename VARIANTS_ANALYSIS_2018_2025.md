## Why Sniper Failed

The Sniper variant's poor performance reveals critical flaws:

### Fatal Flaws
1. **22% unfilled rate** - Missed 29 out of 133 setups
2. **28.8% win rate** - Tight stops get hit too often
3. **Worst drawdown** - $11,621 (3x worse than Conservative)
4. **Only 1.24 profit factor** - Barely profitable
5. **2 losing years** - 2018 (-$6,976), 2022 (-$3,761)

### Why It Doesn't Work
- **50% CE entry** - Price doesn't always retrace that deep
- **Tight stops behind displacement** - Gets stopped out on normal volatility
- **3R target** - Theoretical R:R doesn't materialize due to tight stops

### Lesson Learned
**Precision ≠ Profitability**

While the concept of entering at 50% CE for better R:R is appealing, the execution proves difficult:
- Markets don't always give you the "perfect" entry
- Tight stops are vulnerable to noise
- Missing 22% of opportunities hurts overall performance
