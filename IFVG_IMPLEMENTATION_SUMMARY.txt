📊 FAIR VALUE GAP DETECTION (STRICT WICK RULES)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Bearish FVG (Gap Down):
  • Condition: Low of candle 1 > High of candle 3
  • Gap Zone: [High of candle 3, Low of candle 1]
  • Represents downward price imbalance

Bullish FVG (Gap Up):
  • Condition: High of candle 1 < Low of candle 3
  • Gap Zone: [High of candle 1, Low of candle 3]
  • Represents upward price imbalance

🎯 ENTRY LOGIC (INVERSION STRATEGY)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LONG Entry (Bearish FVG Inversion):
  • Identify a Bearish FVG
  • Wait for price to close ABOVE upper bound (Low of candle 1)
  • Entry: Close price of the inversion candle
  • Time Filter: 02:00 - 06:00 (inclusive)

SHORT Entry (Bullish FVG Inversion):
  • Identify a Bullish FVG
  • Wait for price to close BELOW lower bound (High of candle 1)
  • Entry: Close price of the inversion candle
  • Time Filter: 02:00 - 06:00 (inclusive)

⚙️ TRADE MANAGEMENT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Stop Loss Placement:
  • LONG: Below most recent Swing Low (15 candle lookback)
  • SHORT: Above most recent Swing High (15 candle lookback)
  • Fallback: 0.5% from entry if no swing found

Take Profit Targets:
  • LONG: Previous major Swing High (20 candle lookback)
  • SHORT: Previous major Swing Low (20 candle lookback)
  • Fallback: 1% from entry if no swing found

Swing Detection:
  • Swing High: High > both neighboring highs
  • Swing Low: Low < both neighboring lows
  • Uses local window for pivot identification
