# FVG Size Correlation Analysis (2018-2025)

## Overview

This analysis examines the relationship between FVG (Fair Value Gap) size and FVG quality across all three timeframes. The key question: **Does the size of an FVG correlate with its likelihood to continue or retrace?**

**Analysis Period:** 2018-2025 (8 years)  
**Total FVG Analyzed:** 2,629  
**Timeframes:** 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute

## Executive Summary

### Critical Discovery: **Larger FVG = Better Quality**

Across all timeframes, there is a **strong positive correlation** between FVG size and FVG quality:

- **1-Minute:** Good FVG are 49.77% larger than Bad FVG
- **5-Minute:** Good FVG are 40.97% larger than Bad FVG  
- **15-Minute:** Good FVG are 57.34% larger than Bad FVG
