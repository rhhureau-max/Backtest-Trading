STRATEGY DETAILS
----------------
- Timeframe: 5-minute candles
- Time Filter: London Killzone (01:00-04:00 Chicago time)
- Entry: FVG Inversion + VWAP filter
- Exit: 1:1 Risk/Reward or forced close at 16:00
- Max: 1 trade per day

KEY CONCEPTS
------------
Fair Value Gap (FVG):
- Bearish FVG: Gap down (Low[i-2] > High[i])
- Bullish FVG: Gap up (High[i-2] < Low[i])

FVG Inversion:
- Long: Price closes above bearish FVG + Close > VWAP
- Short: Price closes below bullish FVG + Close < VWAP
